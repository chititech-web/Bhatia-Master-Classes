The Dropper Challenge

Why Most NEET Repeaters Struggle — and How We Fix It

A second attempt doesn't guarantee a better score — only the right strategy does. Here's what holds most droppers back, and how the Parrot Eye program addresses each gap.

No Structured Routine

Most droppers study without a plan. They waste months revisiting the same topics and neglecting weaker areas. Our daily schedule leaves no room for wasted time — every hour is accounted for.

Weak Area Blindness

Students don't know what they don't know. Without regular testing and analysis, weak areas go unnoticed until exam day. Our weekly mock tests with detailed performance reports identify exactly where you need to improve.

Same Mistakes, Different Year

Repeating the same study methods yields the same results. Our faculty analyzes your previous attempt, identifies pattern gaps in your preparation, and builds a customized improvement plan.

No Accountability

Studying alone at home leads to procrastination and loss of motivation. Our small batch size and daily mentoring ensure you stay on track, answerable to both faculty and peers.
