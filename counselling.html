Frequently Asked Questions

NEET counselling is the process through which medical seats are allocated to qualified NEET candidates based on their rank, preferences, and seat availability. Expert counselling ensures you make the right choices during choice filling, understand the difference between AIQ and State quota, and maximize your chances of getting into a good medical college.

All India Quota (AIQ) counselling is conducted by MCC for 15% of seats in government colleges across India. State counselling is conducted by individual state authorities for the remaining 85% state quota seats. Our packages cover both — you don't need separate guidance.
