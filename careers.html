About the Role

We are looking for an enthusiastic and empathetic Academic Counsellor to join Bhatia Master Classes in Freeganj, Ujjain. You will be the first point of contact for students and parents — understanding their academic needs, guiding them toward the right program (JEE, NEET, or Foundation), and helping them take the first step toward their dream career.

Key Responsibilities

  • Counsel students and parents about JEE, NEET, and Foundation programs
  • Handle walk-in and telephonic inquiries at the center
  • Follow up on leads generated through website, WhatsApp, and referrals
  • Maintain accurate records of student interactions in the CRM
  • Coordinate with faculty for demo class scheduling
  • Assist in admission processes and document verification

Who Can Apply?

  • Graduates from any stream (B.Ed / MBA / MSW preferred but not mandatory)
  • Excellent communication skills in Hindi and English
  • Prior experience in counselling, sales, or education is a plus
  • Passionate about guiding students and building relationships
  • Comfortable with basic computer and WhatsApp tools
